Orders

An order is confirmed only when we reply on WhatsApp with the final price and delivery time. Cakes are made only after confirmation. We may decline an order if we cannot make the design in time or if a request is not possible.

Prices and payment

Cake prices are shown per 1 lb (500 g) in Indian rupees on the website. Delivery charge is confirmed on WhatsApp. Party items and custom bundles are priced on WhatsApp.

When you order on the website you pay by UPI, either the full amount now, or 50% as an advance now with the balance on delivery or pickup. If the advance is not paid, we may not start your order.

Delivery

We deliver within about 10 km of Arla, Palampur, or you can collect your order from us. Delivery takes 6–12 hours and depends on the design and your location. Delivery time is confirmed when we confirm your order.

Cancellation, refund & delivery

Every cake is baked only after your order is confirmed. You may cancel before baking begins for a full refund of any advance paid. Once baking has started, the order can no longer be cancelled or refunded. If your cake arrives damaged or is not what was confirmed, send us a photo within 2 hours of delivery and we will replace it or refund you. Small variations in decoration are natural because every cake is handmade.

Ingredients and allergies

Our cakes are eggless, but may contain milk, gluten and nuts. Please tell us about any allergy when you order. We cannot guarantee that a cake is free from traces of other ingredients.
