Questions about eggless wedding cakes

How early should I order a wedding cake?

At least a day ahead for tiered or elaborate cakes. If your date is close, message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is possible.

Can you deliver to the wedding venue?

Yes, inside the 10 km circle around our kitchen in Arla. For Baijnath, Kangra or any address beyond 10 km, message us first so we can confirm delivery and the charge.

How much does a wedding cake cost?

It depends on the weight, flavour and design. Plain cakes start from ₹375 per lb. Wedding and custom designs are priced on WhatsApp before you pay.
