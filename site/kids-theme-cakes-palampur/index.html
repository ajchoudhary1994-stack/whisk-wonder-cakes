Questions about kids theme cakes

Is the cake safe for a child who cannot eat eggs?

Every cake is made without eggs. Cakes may still contain milk, gluten and nuts, so please tell us about any allergy when you order. We cannot guarantee a cake is free from traces of other ingredients.

Can you copy a cartoon or character design?

Send a reference photo on WhatsApp. Small variations in colour and decoration are natural because every cake is made by hand.

Can the balloons and caps come with the cake?

Yes. Party items are delivered together with your cake. Their prices are confirmed on WhatsApp.
