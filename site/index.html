Home Delivery

Cake delivery in Palampur, within 10 km

Home delivery in Palampur and Arla, within a 10 km radius of our kitchen. Your cake — and any party supplies you order with it — arrives fresh at your doorstep. Beyond 10 km (for example Baijnath or Kangra), message us on WhatsApp and we will confirm if we can deliver and the charge.
