How a custom cake works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your reference photo, the date, the weight and the flavour.
  2. We confirm what is possible and the price.
  3. You pay by UPI. Your order is confirmed once we see the payment.
  4. We bake, decorate and deliver, or you collect it from our kitchen in Arla.

Finishes we make

  • Buttercream with piped rosettes, pearls and flowers.
  • Fondant for sculpted and themed designs.
  • Semi-naked finishes with fruit or florals.

Good to know

Because each cake is made by hand, small differences in colour, flowers and decoration are normal. The overall theme and look stay the same as what we confirmed with you.

Questions about custom design cakes

How much does a custom design cake cost?

Plain cakes start from ₹375 per lb, depending on the flavour. Custom designs are priced and confirmed with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

Can I write my own message on the cake?

Yes. Put it in “Message on cake” at checkout or send it on WhatsApp.

How early do I need to order?

We usually deliver within 6–12 hours of confirming your order, depending on the design. For detailed designs, order a day ahead.
